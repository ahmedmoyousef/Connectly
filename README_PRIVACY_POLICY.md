
# 📜 Connectly – Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 2025  
**Last Updated:** May 3, 2025

---

## 1. Introduction

Connectly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information when you use our services.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We do **not collect**, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers.  
However, the app may access:

- 📞 **Phone number input**: Entered by you voluntarily  
- 📋 **Clipboard content**: To offer auto-paste convenience (optional, local-only)  
- 🌐 **Device locale or region**: To pre-select country codes (used locally)  
- 🔗 **Network access**: To open links like WhatsApp or Telegram  

---

## 3. How We Use This Information

All data is handled **locally on your device**. We use this information to:

- Format and validate phone numbers  
- Let you launch chat or call actions  
- Improve your user experience by remembering recent numbers locally (if enabled)  

> We do **not transmit, store, or share your data** with any server or third party.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

No user data is stored on any remote server.  
All operations (e.g., clipboard access, country detection) are processed locally on your device and are **never shared externally**.

---

## 5. What We Don't Do

We **do not**:

- Track your activity  
- Collect contacts or location  
- Store phone numbers in the cloud  
- Use any analytics or ad frameworks  

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Connectly is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.  
Any changes will be reflected within the app or on our support site.
